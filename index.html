The Monster You Hide

It would be a mistake to assume dominion of this world belongs solely to humankind. It doesn’t. It hasn’t. Not ever. Not once.

For nearly two decades, Leonora has survived the slums of Whitechapel. She knows exactly what kind of monsters leave women ripped open at night.

Until a chance meeting with Trinity introduces her to the Otherworld. Ancient. Ruthless. Policed by the fanatical Order of Sparrows—and they want their runaway vampire back.

When the Order’s infamous founder personally descends into Whitechapel to hunt down her new neighbour, Leonora recognises something very human in Trinity’s eyes. A hollow helplessness.

Against all logic, Leonora decides to help Trinity hide, opening a door into her life that can never be closed.

Because the longer Leonora tries to protect Trinity, the more she realises a terrible truth.

The Otherworld is right where Leonora belongs.

The Monster You Wake

How can anyone fight the past? It’s the shadow that follows you. Creeps up on you. And sucks you down into a spiral.

Trinity thought she had escaped the attention of the Order of Sparrows until a lapse in her judgement attracts the very creature she’s been avoiding.

Expecting force or manipulation, the enigmatic founder of the order, Jinx, instead asks for her help.

When a relic dating back to the first dragon is stolen weeks before the dragon king’s second in command’s murder, Jinx knows she needs to send a sparrow into the volatile heartland of dragonkind, Hawaii, to investigate.

But the dragon king will have no one but Trinity.

Thrust into a dangerous maelstrom of dragon fealty, politics, and the unsettling attention of the dragon king himself, the infamous Death at Dawn, needs to find away to defeat a burning monster of smoke and ash before the coming bloodshed consumes both Otherworld and human world together.

About A.R. Willow

A.R. Willow started writing dark stories from an early age that had both teachers and parents scratching their heads.

Today, A.R. Willow lives in Australia, working full-time whilst dedicating her spare time to the fictional worlds and characters running around in her mind.
